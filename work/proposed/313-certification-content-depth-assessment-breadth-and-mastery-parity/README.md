# 313 — Certification Content Depth, Assessment Breadth, and Mastery Parity

> **Status:** Proposed
> **Owner:** `content`
> **Created:** 2026-09-11
> **Depends on:** WI310, WI311

## Why this work item exists

SkillForge already has something many prep products do not: complete published-objective mapping across A+, Network+, and Security+, original lessons/questions, PBQs, mock exams, spaced repetition, local learner state, and no required account.

The gap is not basic coverage. It is **depth**. Competitors can offer much larger banks and broader repetition. SkillForge needs enough original content and simulation diversity that learners practice concepts and troubleshooting rather than memorize a small bank, while keeping its stronger local-first and objective-transparent design.

## Rule

Question count is evidence of breadth, not quality by itself. This work must increase depth without padding the bank with superficial paraphrases or copying live/proprietary exam material.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **CONTENT-001** — publish a dated public-surface parity inventory for bank scale, assessment types, explanations, adaptivity, mocks, flashcards, analytics, and update cadence.
- [ ] **CONTENT-002** — set and meet per-track depth gates while preserving meaningful objective-level cognitive diversity.
- [ ] **CONTENT-003** — add quality/difficulty/misconception metadata and near-duplicate/content validators.
- [ ] **CONTENT-004** — expand original PBQ/simulation formats where the objectives justify them.
- [ ] **CONTENT-005** — improve mock-exam diversity and repeated-exam anti-memorization behavior.
- [ ] **CONTENT-006** — implement a documented, uncertainty-aware mastery/readiness model rather than treating raw accuracy as pass probability.
- [ ] **CONTENT-007** — establish a governed human/agent-assisted content authoring and review pipeline.
- [ ] **CONTENT-008** — perform blind or semi-blind quality review across every track and item type.
- [ ] **CONTENT-009** — use privacy-safe beta/pilot evidence to refine items and learner guidance when available.
- [ ] **CONTENT-010** — derive published content/coverage claims from validated statistics and state parity precisely.

## Non-goals

- Exam dumps or recalled live questions.
- Inflating counts with trivial rewrites.
- Claiming a readiness score is a calibrated pass probability without evidence.
- Requiring a cloud account or telemetry to obtain adaptive study value.
